# Filled shapes and colour preset playtest

Review build, 25 October 2026. Release remains on hold for player review.

Platforms now use solid native vector fills in the existing grey (RGB 0.62), at 0.60 alpha. This is a relative 20% reduction from the previous 0.75 outline alpha. Geometry, collision sizes, movement, jump physics and the 99-level sequence are unchanged.

Settings is available in regular gameplay. Colour Amplifier choices: Normal, Blue Hue, Warm Purple, Setting Sun, Vibrance 2, Invert, Black & White, Dusty Orange, Red Dawn, Bleach Bypass, Toxic, Exposure and Vibrance. Manual selection persists; changing on replay is optional. The camera and UI Toolkit shapes share the packaged colour grades. Invert and monochrome have explicit UI transforms. Settings pauses an active run and resumes it on Done or Escape.

Background stars and falling particles were removed following player review. Collectible dots and their glow now render at twice their former diameter. Pickup distance and scoring remain unchanged.
