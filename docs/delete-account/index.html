Mektra account and data deletion

Mektra is developed by Ark Code Solutions.

Request removal

In Mektra, open Global leaderboard, then Profile and privacy to find your player ID and the deletion request option. You can also email sureshc5551@gmail.com with the subject “Mektra data deletion” and your player ID. Please say whether you want your leaderboard entry removed or your complete leaderboard profile and associated account deleted.

What is removed

The requested leaderboard nickname, best score and leaderboard entry will be removed. For full account deletion, the associated Unity Authentication player account is also deleted. The developer does not retain a copy of these records after completing deletion. Email correspondence may remain to document and answer the request.

Local progress and advertising

Clear Mektra's Android app storage or uninstall it to remove local progress and preferences. Advertising information held by Unity is managed separately through Unity privacy requests; its retention requirements apply. Deleting a leaderboard profile does not remove advertising records.
