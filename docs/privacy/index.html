Mektra Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy describes how the current Android version of Mektra, package com.arkcodesolutions.mektra, handles information.

Information collection and sharing

Mektra does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. The game does not include advertising, analytics, cloud diagnostics, social features or in-app purchases. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, best distance, collected stars, unlocked and selected characters, and sound preferences locally on your device. These records are used to restore your game progress and settings. They are not uploaded to a developer server.

Removing local information

You can remove local game information by clearing Mektra's app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game. Android or device backup services may manage copies of app data under their own settings and privacy policies.

Google Play and device services

Google Play and your device provider may process information when you download the game, manage your device or use their services. That processing is governed by their privacy policies. The developer does not receive personal information through Mektra's gameplay.

Audience

Mektra is intended for players aged 13 and older. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For questions about Mektra or this policy, contact sureshc5551@gmail.com. Information you choose to send by email is used to respond to your request.

Changes

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated. The effective date above identifies the current version.
