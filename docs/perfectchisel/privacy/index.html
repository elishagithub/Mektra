Perfect Chisel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Perfect Chisel is a timing game published by Arkcode Solutions. Contact: sureshc5551@gmail.com.

Local gameplay data

Your best score, selected chisel, music preference, and sound preference are saved on your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes local data. Gameplay does not require an email address, password, or social sign-in.

Online leaderboards

Unity Authentication assigns an anonymous player identifier and may assign a public player name. Unity Leaderboards receives your player identifier and submitted best score. A public player name or abbreviated identifier, rank, and best score are visible to other players. Unity services process technical information needed to provide, secure, and maintain the service. Scores may remain online after uninstalling the game.

Advertising

The Android version includes Unity Ads, with a bottom banner and occasional full-screen ads after completed games. Unity may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, ad views, taps, and diagnostic information to deliver advertising, measure performance, and prevent fraud. Availability and privacy options vary by region and platform. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for its practices, retention, and privacy choices. You can also manage advertising preferences using your device settings.

Sharing

Copy Score places score text on your device clipboard when you choose that action. We do not automatically publish scores to social networks.

Children

Perfect Chisel is a general-audience game and is not primarily directed at children. We do not ask players to provide contact details in the game.

Deletion and other privacy requests

To request deletion of leaderboard data, email sureshc5551@gmail.com with the subject "Perfect Chisel data deletion" and your player identifier. The game's Settings screen lets you copy that identifier. We may request information necessary to locate and verify your record. Local preferences can be removed through device app-storage settings. Unity processes advertising information under its own privacy policy and legal obligations; use the choices described in Unity's policy for requests concerning that information.

Security and changes

Connected services use encrypted network connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We update this page when our practices change.
