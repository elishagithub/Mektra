JumpFlux Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026. JumpFlux is provided by Ark Code Solutions. Contact: sureshc5551@gmail.com.

On-device play

Progress, lifetime stars, unlocked robots, colours and sound settings are saved locally. The game has optional online rankings and advertising, but no purchases. It does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

Optional Star League

Opening the leaderboard connects to Unity Gaming Services. Unity creates an anonymous player identifier to recognize this installation and processes network information required to provide the service. Unity may derive approximate location, such as country, from network information; the game does not request GPS or precise location permission. If you choose to join, your public nickname and lifetime star count are stored by Unity and displayed to other players. These data are used for authentication and rankings, not advertising or sale to data brokers. Network requests use HTTPS.

Read Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Advertising

Android uses Unity LevelPlay to serve banner and interstitial ads. Advertising partners may process device and advertising identifiers, network information including IP address, approximate location, ad interactions, purchase-history or conversion information associated with advertising, and diagnostics to deliver and measure advertising and prevent fraud. Where applicable, consent and privacy choices must be provided before personalized advertising. See Unity’s privacy policy for partner processing and choices.

Sharing

Share saves a gameplay screenshot locally and opens the device share interface with a text result and Play Store link. On macOS, the screenshot is also attached. You choose whether and where to send it. The game does not automatically send messages.

Retention and deletion requests

Local data remains until you clear app data or uninstall. Online records remain until removed. Uninstalling does not automatically remove online records.

To request deletion of your JumpFlux online account and leaderboard record, email sureshc5551@gmail.com with subject “JumpFlux data deletion” and your public nickname, if any. We may request information necessary to identify and verify the profile. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days, except where legal retention obligations apply. You can request online deletion without uninstalling the game.

Audience

The game is intended for ages 13+ and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

Updates

This page will be updated if our data practices change.
