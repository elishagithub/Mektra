App name: Mektra
Category: Game / Arcade
Default language: English (United States)
Package: com.arkcodesolutions.mektra

Short description:
Jump between curved shapes, collect stars and climb through changing worlds.

Full description:
Every jump is a choice. Ride a curved surface, wait for the right angle and launch yourself toward the next shape.

Mektra is a geometric arcade climbing game built around timing, precision and discovery. Travel through a changing journey of smooth curves, multi-lobed shapes, connected hexagons, moving platforms and narrow passages. Some sections give you room to breathe. Others reward a carefully timed long jump or a daring leap from an edge.

Collect stars along the way to replenish your energy and unlock more characters. Watch your health: when it becomes critical, your player slows down. Your distance travelled is your score, so keep climbing and make every jump count.

Discover new ways to move:
• Orbit smooth curves and jump between different geometric shapes.
• Time your way through flowing rows of moving capsules.
• Bounce through narrow passages lined with stars.
• Navigate wedges, zigzag routes and tight hexagonal spaces.
• Stay clear of screen edges, rising lava and swirling black holes.

Enjoy atmospheric worlds, glowing effects and music as you find your rhythm. When a run ends, begin again and use what you learned to climb farther.
