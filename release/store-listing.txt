App name: Mektra
Category: Game / Arcade
Default language: English (United States)
Package: com.arkcodesolutions.mektra

Short description:
Leap through a misty forest, catch glowing fireflies and climb higher.

Full description:
Every jump is a choice. Ride a curved surface, wait for the right angle and launch your glowing orb toward the next shape.

Explore a continuous journey through a misty firefly forest. Smooth organic platforms, moving surfaces, rain-like streams, bouncing plates and narrow passages bring new timing challenges as you climb.

Catch drifting fireflies to unlock colourful orb heroes with matching luminous trails and subtle sparkles. Stretch into a glowing ellipse during flight and leave a pulse of light at takeoff.

Choose your look with Setting Sun, Invert, Black & White and Blue Hue colour presets, plus three forest backgrounds.

Your score records how high you climb. Keep your best distance on your device, or join the optional global leaderboard with a nickname.

There is no health meter or energy drain. Focus on your timing, find your rhythm and make the next leap.
