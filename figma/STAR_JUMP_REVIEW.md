# Star jump revision

All 56 live courses reviewed: 52 received revised star paths; four wedge courses retain stars on their measured physical bounce trajectories. The 320 reward routes include 102 light, 72 medium/long, and 146 edge/risky routes. 48 measured flights exceed 240 game units. Reward trajectories and collection were validated across all eight characters, followed by the endless regression and successful Mac build.

Stars encourage optional launch timing and flight choices. Existing safe connections remain. Platform geometry and jump physics were not changed by this revision.

Figma: 68 design layouts reviewed, 1,840 stars considered and 1,385 repositioned. Gold stars alternate light, medium, edge, recovery and long sections; recovery worlds remain light. Wedge star positions were preserved. New board 23:29888, opening level 23:29896. Prior capsule board 23:21169 is hidden and recoverable. World positions and 390 × 5400 playfields are unchanged.

Figma design paths were checked geometrically, not simulated with live game physics; the extra concept worlds are not part of the 56-course live pack. A cloud connection warning appeared during import. The local SVG is the revision backup.
