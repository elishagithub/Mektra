# Wedge world — Levels 45–48

Figma design revision, column 20:984 at X 8848, Y 200. The previous World_12__Levels_45_48 inside the original board is hidden for recovery. All other world positions are unchanged. Four 390 × 5400 courses remain stacked vertically.

45: Broad banks — wider angular passages, gentler direction changes, recovery spaces.
46: Switchback springs — sharper alternating slopes and tighter gaps, followed by wider chambers.
47: Diamond chambers — offset diamonds divide wider passages; stars reward the open branch.
48: Mixed rhythm — narrow switchbacks, open sections and occasional diamonds.

Each course is authored across its full height, rather than copying one screen five times. Black banks represent bouncy wedge surfaces; circle landings and stars provide route targets. Visually checked in Figma. These are editable design concepts; live game collision and bounce physics have not been updated or validated.
