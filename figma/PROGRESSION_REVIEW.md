# Progression revision

Applied in Figma on 2026-10-09. Board node 21:1763; level 1 node 21:1771.

68 levels across 17 worlds retain their existing positions and 390 × 5400 dimensions. Previous boards are hidden and preserved.

The first progression pass breaks the five identical screen copies through alternating crossings, route widths, landing sizes and original organic shapes in selected open worlds. Bottom-to-top rhythm: easy, medium, difficult, recovery, difficult. Selected precision landings are smaller and stars more compact; introductory and recovery landings remain larger. Latest wedge designs and original organic world are included.

Verified SVG structure, Figma dimensions, original coordinates, overview and level 1 appearance. This is a design revision only. Reachability, fatal misses, collision tolerances and fairness still require game implementation and playtesting. Reflections and size changes reduce repetition but are not a substitute for bespoke routes or gameplay validation.
